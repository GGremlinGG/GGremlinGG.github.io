About the Cause

Poverty is not just the absence of money; it is the quiet erosion of hope. It is the child who goes to sleep with an empty stomach, trying to ignore the ache that hunger brings. It is the parent who skips meals, working endlessly yet still unable to provide enough. Poverty steals choices, dignity, and opportunity, trapping families in a cycle that feels impossible to escape. It turns simple dreams—education, safety, a warm home—into distant luxuries. Behind every statistic is a human story filled with resilience and silent suffering. The weight of uncertainty hangs heavy: Will there be food tomorrow? Will the rent be paid? Will illness mean disaster? And yet, even in the harshest conditions, people hold onto fragments of hope. They cling to love, community, and the belief that life can change. Poverty is not a personal failure; it is a shared responsibility. Until we see it that way, its shadow will continue to fall across generations.
